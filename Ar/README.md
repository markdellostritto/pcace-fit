Datasets:
* data_Ar.xyz - full dataset included crystal structures and clusters
* data_Ar_pair.xyz - includes only Ar pair structures
* data_Ar_cluster.xyz - includes only Ar clusters

Folders:
* fit_sr - direct fitting of PES using short-range (SR) ANN
* fit_sr_pauli - simult. fitting of SR ANN and Pauli ANN for nuclear charge
* fit_sr_pauli_ldamp - simult. fitting of SR ANN, Pauli ANN, and LDamp ANN
